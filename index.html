FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is FitPulse really free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes tracking, the exercise library, goals and the calendar.

Where is my data stored?

In this demo, your data is stored locally in your own browser. Use Settings → Export backup to save it or move it to another device.

I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?

Choose a Beginner program, set your profile, and log your first session. The dashboard will guide you from there.

Can I track nutrition and workouts together?

Absolutely. Calories in and calories out appear side by side on your dashboard and in your analytics charts.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — FitPulse is fully responsive and works on any modern phone, tablet or desktop browser.
